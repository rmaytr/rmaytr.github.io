## The problem

Students learning physics constantly switch between a textbook, a calculator, simulations and a chatbot, and general-purpose AI tutors can hallucinate in STEM explanations.

## What it does

- A **physics-informed AI tutor** built on the **Anthropic API**. A custom physics-grounded system prompt enforces first-principles reasoning and returns structured math rendered with **KaTeX**.
- The tutor gives **hints instead of answers**.
- **Interactive simulations** (spring-mass, projectile motion), **LaTeX formula cards** and an **AI problem generator**, all on one canvas.
- Original pre-rendered physics animations made with **Manim** (Python); more are being added.
- Curriculum planned to cover all **11 units and 88 concepts** of General Physics I.

Phasora is in active development, with working features live.
